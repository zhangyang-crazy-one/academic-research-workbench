# Bounded venue source capsules

V1 source digests remain hashes of accepted retained source bytes. V2 records retain a small structural capsule, with `source_sha256` binding those exact capsule bytes and `provenance.pdf_sha256` recording a separately reviewed full PDF. The full PDF stays transient. Its agent source review decision is `AGENT_REVIEW_RECORDED`, not a human-read attestation or scientific validation.

Each V2 capsule records the six narrative functions once, either as an observation with section, physical PDF locator and paraphrase, or as unknown with a reason and no locator. Figure roles, evidence forms, and observed patterns must agree with the accepted exemplar. Prior review, source metadata, and PDF provenance must also bind exactly. Offline reconstruction rechecks retained capsules and declarations; it cannot reverify PDF bytes that were not retained.

The `learn` CLI accepts source-bound artifacts and reviewed exemplar observations. Callers provide their own reviewed records, explicitly allowed local roots, and transient PDF evidence. Admission checks size, digest, page count, capsule/review/annotation binding, and source-version declarations. Evaluation consumes reviewed candidate samples; only separately promoted candidates appear in applicable advice. These operations do not grant qualification, promotion, consent, narrative approval, network access, or full-text retention.

Accepted author manuscripts use `accepted_author_full_text` with `document_version=accepted_author_manuscript`; author preprints use `preprint_full_text` with `document_version=author_preprint`. Both require repository authority and an explicit relationship note. A related formal DOI or publication URL does not prove that repository PDF bytes match the publisher version. Official publisher PDFs can declare `publisher_version` and `official_publisher` when reviewed. Survey functions may describe taxonomy, comparative synthesis and bounded open questions; they do not imply a new experiment. Bibliometric snapshots remain audit metadata and do not approve or promote a heuristic.
