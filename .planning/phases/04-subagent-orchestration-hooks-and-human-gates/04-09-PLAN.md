<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|---|---|
| sealed parent packet → human assessor | Sealed labels/evidence require controlled access and complete traceability. |
| human conclusion → canonical decision | Human authority can be overbroad, untraceable, or used to bypass a blocker. |
| technical verdict → approval decision | A reviewer can mistake evidence availability for formal qualification. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-04-49 | Spoofing | assessor/adjudicator identity | mitigate | Require separately identified assessor/adjudicator records bound to evidence digests and timestamps. |
| T-04-50 | Tampering | sealed packet/result | mitigate | Verify packet manifest/verdict/raw evidence digests before review and preserve append-only forms. |
| T-04-51 | Repudiation | human conclusion | mitigate | Require rationale, actor, scope, transition, case evidence, and retained disagreement/adjudication history. |
| T-04-52 | Information disclosure | sealed labels | mitigate | Give sealed material only through parent-controlled packet; verify no worker-facing projection receives labels. |
| T-04-53 | Denial of service | unresolved human disagreement | mitigate | Record an explicit BLOCKED outcome and third adjudication route rather than silently finalizing. |
| T-04-54 | Elevation of privilege | approval/waiver | mitigate | D-15/D-16 scope checks prohibit rewrite or direct blocker-to-PASS conversion. |
| T-04-SC | Tampering | package-manager installs | mitigate | No package-manager install is permitted; the review uses already-qualified artifacts. |
</threat_model>
