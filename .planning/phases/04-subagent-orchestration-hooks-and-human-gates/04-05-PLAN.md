<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|---|---|
| worker identity/report → formal panel | A report can claim independence or omit dissent. |
| reviewer envelope → reviewer | Peer/report/synthesis leakage invalidates blind review. |
| human request → canonical gate | A human decision can be overbroad, stale, or attempt to rewrite history. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-04-25 | Spoofing | panel identities | mitigate | Require distinct qualified host identity evidence and block formal mode when proof is absent. |
| T-04-26 | Tampering | reports/finding synthesis | mitigate | Parent admission, immutable source-report digests, complete matrix, and deterministic synthesis. |
| T-04-27 | Repudiation | dissent and human decision | mitigate | Preserve finding provenance and append actor/rationale/scope/evidence-bound decision events. |
| T-04-28 | Information disclosure | blind assignments | mitigate | Strip peer IDs/reports/attempts/synthesis; test delivery envelopes for forbidden fields. |
| T-04-29 | Denial of service | unresolved reports/dissent | mitigate | Required-report/retry exhaustion and critical dissent become explicit blockers rather than unbounded waits. |
| T-04-30 | Elevation of privilege | waiver/approval | mitigate | Enforce one blocker/transition scope and prohibit direct PASS conversion or history rewrites. |
| T-04-SC | Tampering | package-manager installs | mitigate | No package-manager install is permitted; use existing offline dependencies. |
</threat_model>
