## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Source-bound mechanism description
A mechanism figure SHALL bind a versioned accepted source document and retain question, claim, object/state identity, status, phase, explicit input/output types, operations and conditions without synthesizing missing scientific facts.

#### Scenario: Source or type tampering
- **WHEN** a selected source digest changes or an edge joins incompatible ports
- **THEN** rendering fails with a specific reason.

### Requirement: Explainable bounded components
The first renderer SHALL support token/representation, port operations, conditional loops and candidate/state comparison, with an expanded innovation, concrete object chain and supplied manual layout.

#### Scenario: Missing operation details
- **WHEN** the innovation has no sourced internal steps or a feedback edge lacks a stop/return contract
- **THEN** the strict contract rejects the figure.

### Requirement: Independent structural comparison
The checker SHALL compare a frozen source answer with visible SVG labels, token correspondence, freeze state, ports and directed endpoints, and report exact omitted/changed identities.

#### Scenario: Injected faults
- **WHEN** a critical object is removed, an arrow reversed, token changed, freeze state changed or stop condition removed
- **THEN** the checker identifies the corresponding difference without using the generated figure as its answer.

### Requirement: Honest end-to-end evidence
Three synthetic public examples SHALL exercise real accepted sources, parent qualification and replay; optional actual raster outputs SHALL record environment and hashes. Publication SHALL require independent exact-output review.

#### Scenario: Review absent or backend unavailable
- **WHEN** independent review or optional rasterization is missing
- **THEN** the result explicitly remains pending/unavailable and never becomes publication visual PASS.


### Requirement: Separate advisory figure reviewer
The host workflow SHALL use a separate read-only figure-reviewer context with actual source/frozen facts/IR/SVG/final-size PNG/profile/reference inputs, withholding producer scores and selection conclusions. The reviewer SHALL compare content, references and revisions; retain affected identities, concrete evidence, pass conditions and reader retelling. It MUST remain advisory, respect an explicit round budget and retain unresolved findings with human_pending.

#### Scenario: Two-round real review
- **WHEN** the public tasks are revised following independent pixel findings
- **THEN** the second reviewer pass compares actual previous/current pixels, verifies exact bindings and records resolved, partial and new issues without human acceptance or automatic publication PASS.

### Requirement: Explicit normal-figure repair grammar
Source-authored exact coordinate expansion SHALL preserve each operand and
coordinate. A changed rule guard SHALL be isolated from retained clauses. A
once-only initial state SHALL be outside the repeated request path, with distinct
initial and retry input identities. Each conditional predicate SHALL connect to
the output port named by its relation rather than by port inventory order.

#### Scenario: Retry or output-port inventory reorder
- **WHEN** the loop takes two missing results or the output-port inventory is reordered
- **THEN** retry enters the increment/request component without initialization and each predicate remains bound to its own relation/port.

#### Scenario: Repaired local labels and structured source expansion
- **WHEN** the supplied normal fixtures render at the author-defined profile
- **THEN** edge names are intact in reserved route gaps, exact coordinate additions are in separate columns, and the new guard is boxed in aligned before/after expressions; mechanical checks do not imply independent pixel acceptance.
