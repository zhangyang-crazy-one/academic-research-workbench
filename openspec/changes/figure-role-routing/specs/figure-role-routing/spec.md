## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Scoped figure rule routing
The overlay SHALL route an explicit bounded figure brief as audit_view, quantitative, schematic or composite, recording the argument, objects, relations, physical size, selected backend and selected/excluded rules.

#### Scenario: Method diagram
- **WHEN** a schematic brief is routed
- **THEN** statistics-only axes and APA checks are excluded, while source and scientific constraints remain active

### Requirement: Explicit instruction priority
Scientific truth and output safety MUST remain invariant; verified venue constraints and explicit author choices SHALL precede matching role defaults, and optional style templates SHALL NOT become universal requirements. Strict box rules SHALL require explicit selection.

#### Scenario: Depth and box choices
- **WHEN** a sourced spatial mechanism or explicit strict-box brief is routed
- **THEN** meaningful depth is permitted for the mechanism and strict-box instructions appear only for the selected box workflow; misleading quantitative perspective is rejected

### Requirement: Honest backend support
Routing SHALL report actual fixed font/layout/geometry capability gaps without claiming rendering, evidence admission or visual acceptance.

#### Scenario: Mixed main figure
- **WHEN** a composite requests multiple panels from a single-panel v1 backend
- **THEN** the request reports unavailable and each child retains its own role rules
