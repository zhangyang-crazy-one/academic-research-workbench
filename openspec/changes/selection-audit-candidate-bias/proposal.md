## Why

A retained search can favor one conclusion through query, ranking, deduplication, reading order, and early stopping. ARS already records the first three, but a final citation list cannot prove what the author read or why they stopped. An offline, provenance-bound view is needed before treating selection bias as observable.

## What Changes

- Add `arw selection-audit export` for read-only, bounded receipts from parent-accepted ARS plan/input/ledger and optional explicit reading trace artifacts.
- Add `arw selection-audit replay` for exact receipt verification or a public self-authored synthetic candidate-pool demonstration of paired same-budget ordering.
- Keep scientific stance unknown unless an accepted human reading assertion supplies it; the synthetic fixture states labels as artificial assumptions.
- Report descriptive coverage and sensitivity, and a separate enumerated SRS teaching example. No propensity or production correction is fabricated.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `selection-audit-candidate-bias`: Trace candidate selection and demonstrate ordering sensitivity without changing manuscripts or ARS contracts.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

The Python CLI and first-party audit module, tests, fixture, and documentation change. Bundled ARS is read through its existing offline normative validator. No provider, model, network, or new dependency is used.
