## Why

The selected paper strategy currently survives handoff, but outlines, argument blueprints, and drafts can still omit its functions or carry ungrounded claims. Acceptance must check the retained content and its narrative version.

## What Changes

- Add a versioned, provider neutral realization contract for outline, blueprint, and draft artifacts with exact text spans and a claim–contribution–evidence–boundary graph.
- Check coverage, graph links, narrative freshness, ordered first appearances, and actual retained source bytes during artifact acceptance.
- Route explained interleaving and semantic support to human review while rejecting mechanical failures.
- Document that existing narrative plans remain readable and scientific findings remain open to revision.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `narrative-realization`: Content bound realization contracts and deterministic acceptance checks for paper artifacts.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

The canonical schema registry, artifact acceptance runtime, paper writing protocol, documentation, and narrative tests change. No external model or service is required.
